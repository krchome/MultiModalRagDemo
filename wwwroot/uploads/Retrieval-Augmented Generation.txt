Retrieval-Augmented Generation, or RAG, is a technique that combines information retrieval with generative AI.

Instead of relying only on the model's internal knowledge, a RAG system retrieves relevant content from external documents.

The retrieved content is then provided to the language model as context.

This allows the model to generate more accurate and grounded responses.

A typical RAG pipeline includes document upload, text extraction, text chunking, embeddings, vector storage, semantic search, and final answer generation.
