A Quiet Morning

The sun rose slowly over the quiet town, painting the cobblestones
with a soft, golden light. Emma walked down the lane, the collar of
her coat turned up against the chill. She paused at the old bookshop,
peering through the fogged window at a stack of tales that smelled
of dust and distant summers.

Inside, Mr. Larkin smiled without surprise. "Every town needs a
storyteller," he said, and pushed a small, worn book toward her.

Emma opened it and found, between its pages, a map to places she'd
only dreamed of and a note that read: "Begin where you are."

She stepped back into the street, the map warm in her hands, and
walked toward the day she had yet to write.
